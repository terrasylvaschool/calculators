Alcohol Calculators

Three tools for working out dilutions, fortifications, and blends of alcoholic menstruums.

Fill in the blanks in any sentence. The shaded field fills in as soon as the other values are entered.

Use consistent units for volume across a sentence. Whatever unit you enter (mL, ounces, cups) is the unit of the result.

Dilute with water

I have % alcohol, and I want to dilute it with water down to units of % alcohol. I need to combine — units of the alcohol with — units of water.

example: I have 95% alcohol on hand, and I need 20 ounces of 65% alcohol for the tincture I'm making.

formula: Units of alcohol = target % × target units ÷ starting %
The rest is water.

Raise the proof

I have units of % alcohol, and I want to raise it to % alcohol. The alcohol I am adding is %. I need to add — units of my stronger alcoholic solution.

example: I'm making a mushroom double-extract. I have 22 ounces of reduced decoction (0% alcohol), and I need to raise it to 25% alcohol for preservation, using my tincture of the same mushroom, which is 80% alcohol.

formula: Units to add = units on hand × (target % − current %) ÷ (added % − target %)

Combine two solutions

I have units of % alcohol, and units of % alcohol. If I combine the two, I will have — units of — % alcohol.

example: I have 6 ounces of Reishi double-extract, which is 25% alcohol. If I add two ounces of Milky Oats tincture to it (50% alcohol), what alcohol % will the combination be?

formula: Resulting % = (units of first × first % + units of second × second %) ÷ total units

Percentages are by volume (ABV). Volumes are treated as simply additive here; real alcohol–water mixtures contract slightly when combined, so expect the final volume to come in slightly under the calculated figure.
